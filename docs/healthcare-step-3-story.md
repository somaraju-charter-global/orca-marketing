# Healthcare Step 3 Story

# 1. Core Story

## Vertical Thesis

Healthcare providers have invested heavily in Electronic Health Records (EHR), Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP), and Revenue Cycle Management platforms. While these systems capture clinical and operational data, much of the work required to keep healthcare operations moving still happens outside those systems.

Documents are exchanged through email, approvals are tracked in spreadsheets, compliance evidence is collected manually, and teams spend valuable time following up on missing information. These disconnected workflows create delays, increase administrative burden, and introduce compliance risk.

Orcaworks governs this operational execution layer by bringing together workflow context, documents, approvals, evidence, and user actions while allowing healthcare organizations to continue using their existing systems.

---

## Why This Matters Now

Healthcare organizations are operating with lean clinical and administrative teams while facing increasing regulatory requirements, growing documentation demands, and pressure to improve financial performance.

As patient volumes increase and reimbursement processes become more complex, manual coordination around approvals, documentation, and compliance becomes more difficult to manage. Organizations need practical ways to improve execution without replacing their existing technology investments.

---

## The Problem

Healthcare operations involve constant coordination between clinicians, administrative teams, compliance officers, revenue cycle specialists, and external partners.

Common operational challenges include:

- Missing clinical documentation
- Delayed insurance approvals
- Manual provider credential verification
- Audit evidence scattered across multiple systems
- Email-based approval processes
- Limited visibility into workflow status
- Repeated follow-up for incomplete information

These challenges slow patient care, delay reimbursements, increase compliance risk, and consume valuable staff time.

---

## Why Existing Systems Are Not Enough

Core healthcare platforms such as EHRs and ERP systems are excellent systems of record. However, they do not manage every task required to complete operational work.

Teams still rely on emails, spreadsheets, shared folders, and manual reminders to:

- Collect supporting documents
- Request approvals
- Track missing information
- Escalate delayed work
- Maintain audit evidence
- Coordinate between departments

The execution layer around these systems remains highly manual.

---

## How Orcaworks Helps

Orcaworks helps healthcare organizations govern operational workflows without replacing their existing technology.

### Workflow Context

Orcaworks connects documents, approvals, owners, evidence, workflow status, and operational history to create complete workflow visibility.

### Declarative Agents

Healthcare teams can define workflow rules that automatically validate documents, request missing information, route approvals, escalate delays, and record decisions.

### Bring the Bot to the User

Instead of requiring users to switch applications, Orcaworks delivers the next required action directly within email, Microsoft Teams, web applications, or operational portals.

# 2. Strategic Pillars

## Pillar 1 — Prior Authorization

### Story

Prior authorization remains one of the most document-intensive workflows in healthcare. Clinical teams collect medical records, physicians approve requests, insurance providers review submissions, and administrative staff continuously monitor status updates.

Missing documentation or delayed approvals often postpone patient care and reimbursement.

Orcaworks governs this workflow by coordinating documents, routing approvals, tracking exceptions, and keeping stakeholders informed throughout the process.

### Selection Logic

Market urgency: High

Customer business value: High

Operational pain: High

Why this is pilotable:

The workflow is repetitive, measurable, and involves multiple approvals and documents.

### Key Workflows

- Clinical document collection
- Insurance approval routing
- Status follow-up

### Orcaworks Value Proposition

Improve workflow visibility, reduce manual follow-up, and ensure that approvals move efficiently through each stage.

### Metrics to Measure

- Authorization turnaround time
- Missing documentation rate
- Approval completion time

# 3. Closing Story

Healthcare organizations already have strong clinical systems, but operational work still depends on manual coordination across documents, approvals, and departments.

Rather than replacing EHR or ERP platforms, Orcaworks governs the execution work that happens around those systems. By starting with one measurable workflow, such as Prior Authorization or Clinical Documentation, organizations can improve efficiency, reduce administrative burden, and build a foundation for broader operational transformation.
