# 4. Campaign Narrative

## Vertical Thesis

Healthcare providers have invested heavily in EHR, ERP, and clinical systems, but operational work surrounding documentation, approvals, compliance, and coordination still relies on manual effort.

## Core Problem

Critical workflows become delayed because documents, approvals, evidence, and ownership are spread across multiple systems, emails, and spreadsheets.

## Why Existing Systems Are Not Enough

Core systems store clinical and operational records, but they do not govern the execution work that happens around those records.

## Why Orcaworks Fits

Orcaworks provides workflow context, declarative agents, and user-facing execution to coordinate documents, approvals, evidence collection, and follow-up without replacing existing healthcare systems.

## Pilot Start Motion

Begin with a single workflow such as Prior Authorization, validate measurable improvements, and then expand to additional healthcare operational workflows.
