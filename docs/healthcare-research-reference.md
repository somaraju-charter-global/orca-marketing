# Healthcare Research Reference

## 1. Executive Summary

Healthcare organizations are under increasing pressure to improve operational efficiency while maintaining high standards of patient care, regulatory compliance, and financial performance. Most hospitals and healthcare providers already have core systems such as EHRs, ERPs, and revenue cycle platforms, but much of the work surrounding these systems still depends on emails, spreadsheets, shared documents, manual approvals, and status follow-ups.

Orcaworks helps govern these workflows by providing context, declarative workflow automation, and user-facing execution without replacing existing systems.

## 9. Research Summary

Healthcare organizations have mature core systems but still depend on manual coordination around documentation, approvals, compliance, and exception handling. These operational gaps create strong opportunities for Orcaworks to provide governed workflow execution without replacing existing systems.
